About SFI

Spectre Fantasy Intelligence is an independently developed personal fantasy football project operated by SpectreNJ.

The Spectre Data Engine collects fantasy football information from the Sleeper API and uses Google Drive to store and update intelligence files authorized by the project operator.

SFI uses the Google Drive file-specific permission scope to manage application-authorized files. The platform is intended for private personal use and is not a publicly available fantasy service.

SFI is not affiliated with Google, Sleeper, or the National Football League.
